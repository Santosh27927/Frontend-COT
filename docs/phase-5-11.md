# Review phases 5–11

This guide describes the selected prototype, not a native app release. The user authorized implementing the settled plan on 25 November 2026. The consolidated plan and decision provenance are preserved in the [selected planning archive](planning/phase-5-11/README.md). The [current checkpoint](project-checkpoint.md) covers all phases and pending limitations. Current executable types and this handoff supersede older proposals.

## Suggested review order

1. Sign in, inspect the landscape village, and open learning. Normal gameplay contains no device status badge. Try PIN letters, punctuation and a fifth digit; typing feedback and service validation are independent.
2. Complete three market scenarios to earn the ETC collector, then the GTC, IOC/FOK and stop-limit lessons. All twelve spot passes unlock the converter and perpetual lessons. Stop-limit completion earns the BTC collector. There is no SOL collector.
3. Between demonstrations, inspect current simulated asset prices. Choose converter source and destination. There is no editable amount. The service validates at least $50 source value, full destination storage including bonus, quote ownership/expiry and cooldown. Failed preview/confirmation does not consume cooldown.
4. Review Town Hall's payment quote, then start its 12-hour build. Inspect original paid units and finish time. Cancel only after reviewing the loss and acknowledging it. Retry/reload preserves receipts. Restart means a new full payment and timer.
5. Advance the separate development world clock to test offline completion. After Town Hall 2, inspect each collector's opposite-asset payment. Old rate/store continues until the accepted completion boundary, then the accepted new effect applies.
6. Observe optional questions in the village hub after each lesson. A wrong answer explains the correct answer and allows free retries. The first correct completion pays 5 on attempt one or 2.50 on any later attempt; repeated completion pays nothing. Correct answer and Explanation have separate blocks.
7. Inspect wallet caps and pending credits. Full optional destinations reject. Collector transfer is partial. Earned refunds/profits/rewards overflow safely to pending credit. Claim transfers existing value, never another reward or PnL.
8. Inspect assistance status. Ordinary cadence is intentionally unavailable. The finite development preset permits at most one eligible extra $100 claim. The service rechecks funds, recovery routes, offer and policy at confirmation.

Completed scenario practice has an isolated wallet and reports. New manual village actions are blocked throughout both real and practice demonstrations, including after navigating back to the village. Accepted automatic fills, production and construction continue.

## Configuration and deliberate limits

| Policy area     | Initial value and update rule                                                                                                          |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Learning        | 180-second deadline, 60-second guided fill, 30-second stop activation, 20-second review; frozen per accepted scenario                  |
| Cash/asset caps | USD 2,000; ETC 350; SOL 20; BTC explicitly unlimited gameplay storage; units fixed across market movement                              |
| Converter       | Nominal $50, +25%, zero fees, 30-second quote, 172,800-second lock after success                                                       |
| Slots           | Town Hall levels 1/2: 20 simultaneous constructions, one per building; only three buildings exist in this prototype                    |
| Construction    | Town Hall $100 equivalent; ETC upgrade 0.001 BTC; BTC upgrade 10 ETC; 43,200 seconds each; 50% original-unit cancellation refund       |
| Questions       | Stable IDs/revisions; first-correct reward 5 or 2.50; accepted attempt counts never reset by relogin/text changes                      |
| Assistance      | Cash strictly below 50, extra 100; ordinary cadence/repeat configuration intentionally pending; one-claim development fixture          |
| Production      | Level 1 ETC 1/h store 50 and BTC 0.0001/h store 0.005; level 2 doubles both. Existing stores need an explicit effective-time migration |

Settings are service data. Future levels need explicit definitions; missing settings do not mean free, zero or unlimited. The future back office should edit the same validated provider with staff authorization, audit, preview and rollback. It must not disable exact arithmetic, owner checks, once-only receipts or conservation. No staff UI is built here.

Final production art, renderer choice and native lifecycle/security are deferred to the design/platform teams. Assistance cadence remains the only deliberate economy tuning decision without ordinary defaults. No new product question blocks testing the selected browser behavior.
